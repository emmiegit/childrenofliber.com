This is a fan implementation of the childrenoflibor.com, as described in SCP-3455.

You should just read the article, but it describes an anomalous period of time which adds an "extra month" of 47 days to the year, called Liberary. This website is said to show a countdown until the next occurrence of the month outside the time period itself.

Its source code is available on GitHub: emmiegit/childrenofliber.com.
